# Grey Simulator

An endless caretaking sandbox with a fully modeled, textured and skeletal baby character in a 3D nursery. Static HTML/JS; no build step. Serve the repository root (for example `python -m http.server 8000`) or enable GitHub Pages on main/root. An internet connection is required for the pinned Three.js 0.160.1 CDN modules. Open through HTTP(S); GLB loading is not supported under file://.

## Camera

Tap **Camera** and drag one finger to orbit around the crib, including side and rear views. Tap Camera again to resume caretaking. Desktop also supports Shift-drag or right-drag, plus scroll to zoom. **Center** restores the starting viewpoint.

## Play

Pet & tickle: drag slowly over the head to soothe or rub the body for giggles. Peek-a-boo: hold the scene/on-screen button or Space, then release after 1.2–2.5 seconds. Rattle: shake with a steady drag rhythm. Plush: tap to squeeze. Mobile: select and tap for a 20-second lullaby. Bubbles / boo: initially a playful sound; soap bubbles unlock at level 4.

Bond saves locally on this browser. Switching actions grants variety bonuses. Levels 2–5 add colored mobile stars, bunny ears/rattle color, bubbles and sunset lighting. Audio starts muted; tap Sound off to enable it. Optional `assets/coo.mp3`, `assets/giggle.mp3` and `assets/laugh.mp3` replace synthesized voices. Missing samples fall back to synthesis.

## Character and license

The active character is **“Sunlit Little Smile,”** generated by Meshy from Grey's photograph and loaded from `assets/baby/grey-realistic.glb` (6,313,216 bytes). Blender processing reduced it from approximately 631k to 45k triangles, welded duplicate vertices, downscaled textures to 1024, and added a custom 19-bone skeleton matching the game's exact bone names. The truncated download's missing normal-map tail was repaired before processing. Model provenance, modifications and required **Meshy CC BY 4.0 attribution** are documented in [SOURCE.md](assets/baby/SOURCE.md#grey-realisticglb). Model created with Meshy – CC BY 4.0 License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

The realistic model's rest pose is already seated. `preseated=true` deliberately skips the `aim()` seating calls; future pose work must build on that rest pose. Height normalization, centering and hip-weighted mattress grounding remain in place. Breathing, gaze tracking, arm bounces and leg kicks run procedurally from the captured rest pose. This GLB contains no animation clips or separate eye meshes: `babyMixer` is null by design, and textured eyes do not blink independently.

Known source-texture quirks include dark hair streaks on one side and mottling under the chin. These are texture artifacts rather than rig defects.

Both `assets/baby/grey.glb` (the earlier painted character) and `assets/baby/baby-rigged.glb` (the Grab3D toddler) remain untouched as manual fallbacks. The stock model retains its [source disclosure](assets/baby/SOURCE.md) and [license](assets/baby/LICENSE.txt). Restoring a fallback requires reviewing its facing and setting `preseated=false` to restore the original seating aims. Loading failures display the existing error/retry UI rather than silently changing characters.

## Validation

Chromium checks cover model load and skeleton, seated bounds, side/rear orbit and reset, interaction feedback, and a 390×844 touch-emulated layout. Run `node tests/rigged.cjs`, `node tests/loading.cjs`, and `node tests/browser.cjs` with Playwright and Chromium installed and the local server running. Real iPhone Safari, audio listening, and sustained mobile frame-rate testing remain manual validation.

Seat grounding uses the lowest contact patch of hip-weighted skinned vertices, rather than the overall model's lowest vertex. The pelvis remains fixed vertically during reactions; only the upper body and limbs animate. A soft shadow stays centered beneath the seated pelvis.
